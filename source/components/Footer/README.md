# Footer

Site footer on the brand gradient under a 50% black veil: top nav, three link columns, the 3HCloud promo, legal row; contacts, logo and socials on the right.

- Props: `variant` = `desktop` (1440, 12px text, default) | `wide` (1920 screens, container 1530, 14px text, larger logo) | `mobile` (414: contacts first, centered socials, link row, legal; faded mark in the corner); `nav`, `columns` (arrays of labels), `mobileLinks`, `contacts`, `special` (`false` hides Special Offers), `promo` (`false` hides the 3HCloud block).
- Pick the variant by breakpoint: `mobile` under 768, `desktop` up to 1680, `wide` above.
